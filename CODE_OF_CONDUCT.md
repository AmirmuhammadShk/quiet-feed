# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project participants should communicate respectfully, welcome constructive feedback, and focus discussion on improving Quiet Feed. Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this standard.

Maintainers should handle reports consistently, protect reporters' privacy, and avoid conflicts of interest.
